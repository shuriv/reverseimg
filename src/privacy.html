Privacy policy

ReverseIMG is built to keep your searches private.

What ReverseIMG collects

Nothing. ReverseIMG has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or developer servers.

What stays on your device

Settings and recent searches stay in Firefox local storage. Temporary image data stays in IndexedDB for up to 15 minutes and is deleted automatically.

Where your searches go

When you start a search, your image or query is sent directly to the search engine you choose. That engine's privacy policy applies.

No tracking or selling

ReverseIMG does not track you, profile you, or sell data.

Permissions

ReverseIMG requests only menus, storage, activeTab, and scripting. It does not request host permissions and does not read pages in the background.

AMO data collection declaration

The add-on declares searchTerms and websiteContent to Mozilla Add-ons. The developer does not receive or retain them.

Retention and deletion

Recent searches, temporary images, and settings remain until you clear them, reset them, or uninstall the extension. The developer has no server-side data to delete.

Children

ReverseIMG is a general-purpose search utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the project repository and the extension privacy page, with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact matthew on GitHub at https://github.com/shuriv.

Scope

This policy applies to the ReverseIMG Firefox extension and its bundled pages. It does not apply to the websites of the search engines or other third parties.
