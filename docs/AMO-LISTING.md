## English (en)

### Summary

Reverse-search any image or search by keyword with Google Lens, Bing, Yandex, TinEye and SauceNAO.

### Description

ReverseIMG adds two kinds of image search to Firefox.

**Reverse image search** — right-click an image and send it to Google Lens, Bing, Yandex, TinEye, or SauceNAO. You can also open the popup, upload a file, drag and drop an image, paste from the clipboard, or enter an image URL.

**Keyword image search** — search Google Images, Bing Images, Yandex Images, DuckDuckGo, or Brave Images from the toolbar popup.

Features:

- Choose a default engine, enable or disable engines, and reorder them.
- Optional full right-click submenu.
- Search selected page text for images.
- Light, dark, and system themes.
- Recent keyword searches, stored locally.
- Export and import settings.
- Eight languages: English, Russian, French, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, German, Brazilian Portuguese, and Hindi.
- No accounts, analytics, advertising, or developer servers.

Privacy:

Searches go directly to the search engine you choose. ReverseIMG does not request host permissions and does not read pages in the background. Settings and recent searches stay in your Firefox profile. Temporary image data is kept for at most 15 minutes during the upload handoff and is then removed.

Created by matthew — https://github.com/shuriv

Engine names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. ReverseIMG is not affiliated with Google, Microsoft, Yandex, TinEye, or SauceNAO.
