# Privacy policy

ReverseIMG is built to keep your searches private.

## What ReverseIMG collects

Nothing. ReverseIMG has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or developer servers.

## What stays on your device

Settings and recent searches stay in Firefox local storage. Temporary image data stays in IndexedDB for up to 15 minutes and is deleted automatically.

## Where your searches go

When you start a search, your image or query is sent directly to the search engine you choose. That engine's privacy policy applies.

## No tracking or selling

ReverseIMG does not track you, profile you, or sell data.

## Permissions

ReverseIMG requests only menus, storage, activeTab, and scripting. menus adds the right-click items, storage saves your settings and recent searches, activeTab reads the image you explicitly right-clicked, and scripting reads blob: image data after you choose a search. ReverseIMG does not request host permissions and does not read pages in the background.

## AMO data collection declaration

The add-on declares searchTerms and websiteContent to Mozilla Add-ons. These categories describe data sent directly to the search engine you choose. The developer does not receive or retain it.

## Retention and deletion

Recent searches stay until you clear them or uninstall the extension. Temporary image entries are removed after the upload handoff or after 15 minutes. Settings stay until you reset them or uninstall. The developer has no server-side data to delete.

## Children

ReverseIMG is a general-purpose search utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the project repository and the extension privacy page, with a new date. Material changes may also be noted in the release notes.

## Contact

Questions? Contact matthew on GitHub at https://github.com/shuriv.

## Scope

This policy applies to the ReverseIMG Firefox extension and its bundled pages. It does not apply to the websites of Google, Microsoft, Yandex, TinEye, SauceNAO, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Mozilla, GitHub, or any other third party.
